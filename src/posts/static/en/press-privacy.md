# Privacy notice for journalists and media contacts

How we handle your data when we contact you about our research.

## Who we are

The Boat4you Group press programme is run by **Cusmanich d.o.o.**, Vrboran 37, 21000 Split, Croatia (MB 4026365, OIB 87394862517), the controller of your data. We publish research on yacht charter prices and travel trends and share it with journalists.  
**Contact:** [press@boat4you.com](mailto:press@boat4you.com)

## What we hold about you

Your name, the publication you write for, your role or beat, your work email address, where we found that address (for example your outlet's staff page or a byline) and the date, the emails we exchanged with you, and whether you replied, declined or published a story.

## Where it comes from

Only from sources where you or your publication made it public for professional contact: your outlet's website, article bylines, your professional profiles, or directly from you.

## Why we use it, and on what legal basis

To send you research and story ideas relevant to what you cover, to answer you, and to see whether our research was covered. We rely on our legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)) in sharing our research with the press and in having our brands and studies covered and credited. We have weighed that interest against yours: we use only work contact details that you or your outlet published, we contact only journalists who cover the topic, and we email you one to one, at most once per study plus one follow-up. You can ask us for a summary of that assessment.

## How we email you

Plain text, one to one, with no tracking pixels and no link tracking. We make no automated decisions about you.

## Who we share it with

We do not sell, rent or pass on your data to anyone for their own use. It is stored on our own servers in the European Union (hosted by Hetzner Online GmbH) and on our own mail server. We use Claude, an AI assistant made by Anthropic PBC, to help us find journalists who cover relevant topics and to draft our emails, so Anthropic processes your professional contact details on our behalf. Anthropic is based in the United States, so this involves a transfer of those details outside the EU.

## How long we keep it

We delete your contact record and our email history with you automatically once 24 months have passed since our last email to you (the clean-up runs monthly). Copies of our emails in the press@ mailbox are deleted on the same schedule. If you ask us to stop, or an email to you bounces, we keep only your email address on a do-not-contact list, indefinitely, so that we never email you again; we keep it on the basis of our legitimate interest in respecting your objection.

## Your rights

You can object to being contacted at any time: just reply asking us to stop, or write to [press@boat4you.com](mailto:press@boat4you.com). We add your address to our do-not-contact list as soon as we read your reply, normally within two working days, and we send you nothing more, not even a follow-up. You can also ask for access to your data, its correction or deletion, or restriction of processing; we answer requests within one month. You can complain to the Croatian data protection authority ([AZOP, azop.hr](https://azop.hr)) or to the authority in your country.

## If you are outside the EU

We apply this notice to everyone we contact. In the UK you can also complain to the ICO ([ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk)), in Australia to the OAIC ([oaic.gov.au](https://www.oaic.gov.au)) and in New Zealand to the Privacy Commissioner ([privacy.org.nz](https://www.privacy.org.nz)). If we asked for your consent before sending you anything, you can withdraw it at any time by replying.

For everything else, see our general [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy).

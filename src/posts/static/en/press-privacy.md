# Privacy notice for journalists and media contacts

How we handle your data when we contact you about our research.

## Who we are

Boat4you Group is operated by **Cusmanich d.o.o.**, Vrboran 37, 21000 Split, Croatia (MB 4026365, OIB/VAT 87394862517), the controller of your data. We publish research on yacht charter prices and travel trends and share it with journalists.  
**Contact:** [press@boat4you.com](mailto:press@boat4you.com)

## What we hold about you

Your name, the publication you write for, your role or beat, your work email address, where we found that address (for example your outlet's staff page or a byline) and the date, and a record of the emails we exchanged with you.

## Where it comes from

Only from sources where you or your publication made it public for professional contact: your outlet's website, article bylines, your professional profiles, or directly from you.

## Why we use it, and on what legal basis

To send you research and story ideas relevant to what you cover, and to answer you. We rely on our legitimate interest in sharing newsworthy data with the press (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)); we have balanced that interest against your rights and only contact journalists whose work is relevant to the topic. We email you one to one, at most once per study plus one follow-up.

## Who we share it with

No one. We do not sell, rent or pass on contact data. It is stored on servers in the European Union, and the companies that host them process it only on our instructions.

## How long we keep it

As long as you cover relevant topics, and no longer than 24 months after our last contact with you. If you ask us to stop, we keep only your email address on a suppression list so that we never contact you again.

## Your rights

You can object to being contacted at any time: just reply "no thanks" or write to [press@boat4you.com](mailto:press@boat4you.com), and we will stop immediately. You can also ask for access to your data, its correction or deletion, or restriction of processing. You can complain to the Croatian data protection authority ([AZOP, azop.hr](https://azop.hr)) or to the authority in your country.

## If you are outside the EU

If you are in the UK, the US, Australia, New Zealand or elsewhere, the same rules apply to you: we only use professional contact details that you or your outlet published, we identify ourselves in every email, and every email lets you opt out.

For everything else, see our general [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy).
